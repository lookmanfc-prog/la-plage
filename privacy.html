Privacy policy.

LA PLAGE is a small beachwear brand run by students in Saigon, Vietnam. This page explains what personal information we collect on this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Questions: laplage.vn@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Private access list: your email address, if you sign up to hear about new drops.
  • Account: your username, name, password (stored encrypted by our login provider; we never see it), Gmail address, and, if you add them, your phone number and shipping address.
  • Pre-orders: the pieces, sizes and colours you choose, your name, phone, Gmail, shipping address, any note you add, the totals, and the order status.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the site.

We do not take card details on this website. Payment is arranged with you directly.

How we use it

  • To make, pack and deliver your pre-order, and to contact you about it.
  • To send pre-order confirmations to your Gmail.
  • To email you about new drops, only if you joined the Private access list.
  • To keep basic records of our sales.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the shop, and only for that purpose:

  • Google Firebase stores accounts, pre-orders and the Private access list.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • EmailJS and Gmail send pre-order emails.
  • A delivery company receives your name, phone and address to deliver your order.
  • The page also loads fonts from Google Fonts.

These services may store data on servers outside Vietnam. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies and storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. When you sign in, your browser keeps a small piece of data so you stay signed in; signing out removes it.

How long we keep it

We keep account and pre-order information while your account is open and for as long as we need it for our sales records. You can leave the Private access list at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to take you off the Private access list. Email laplage.vn@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can. You can also update your contact details and address yourself in your account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
